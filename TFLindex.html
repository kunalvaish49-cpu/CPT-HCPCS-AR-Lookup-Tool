What is the Timely Filing Limit (TFL)?

The Timely Filing Limit is the time duration from service rendered to patients and submitting claims to the insurance companies. If a patient gets services on the 1st of any month there is a time limit to submit the claim to the insurance company for reimbursement — typically 30 days to 1 year depending on the payer.

The billing agency's responsibility is to submit claims within the timely filing limit, otherwise claims will be denied due to timely filing exceeded (CO-29).

This tool consolidates three data sources: TFL Code sheet (insurance-specific timely filing windows), Payer ID List (transaction types and identifiers), and Master Sheet (addresses, portals, appeal info). Addresses marked ✨ Suggested are recommended standard billing addresses based on publicly available payer information.
